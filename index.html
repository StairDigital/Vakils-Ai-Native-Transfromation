05Sandoz Global ePI RFI: Proof From a Live BidPOC shortlist

Proof from a live bid.

Between September and October 2026, Vakils answered a global pharma RFI, built working ePI evidence and demonstrated it to Sandoz, with STAIR independently verifying every result. It is the first live test of the Vakils 2.0 thesis.

The opportunity

  • A global generics leader asks for a governed ePI platform: the Sandoz Global ePI RFI covers structured content, governance, enterprise architecture, market-specific publication and 7 cross-functional scenarios.
  • Vakils bid under its own name: the Life Sciences, Regulatory and Technical teams fronted the response and built the evidence; STAIR provided specialist architecture, standards and governance support and the independent verification.
  • A short, defined path: 3 vendors shortlisted after RFI scoring; POC from mid-November 2026 to January 2027; RFP about 1 month later; implementation by 1 April 2027. EMA ePI go-live may come by year end.

What Vakils built and showed

  • A working EU FHIR ePI engine at prototype level: it reads official EMA samples, applies controlled edits, generates FHIR documents and checks them with the standard HL7 FHIR Validator against the EU ePI Implementation Guide, with a governed workflow, hash-chained audit trail and open export around it.
  • 3 evidence packs with reproduction kits: E21 EU FHIR tests (107 files), E22 export and importability tests (73 files) and E24 scenario demonstrations (200 files), each with SHA-256 checksums so anyone can re-run them.
  • A demonstration to Sandoz on 21 October 2026: 3 of the 7 scenarios shown live from recorded runs, with 10 design-concept screens of the intended enterprise experience, and every claim kept inside its evidence boundary.

06AI-Led Internal Audit and Regulatory ComplianceProposed

Controls monitored continuously.

Vakils will run internal audit and compliance through AI dashboards that monitor controls continuously, with every finding traceable to a source and every decision taken by a named person.

ScopeSTAIR's Scope Agent, already live in production, turns the engagement letter and risk register into a quarter-wise annual audit scope.
IngestSOPs, checklists, audit reports, control matrices, defect logs, VAQA trails and finance data feed 1 system of record.
AnalyseFindings, root causes, recurring patterns and red flags extracted on a standard taxonomy (Critical, Major, Minor) with confidence scores.
MonitorLive dashboards track quality gates, time-stamped checklist evidence, build-freeze compliance, rework and escaped defects.
DecideThe AI advises only: it never approves exceptions, overrides controls or suppresses findings. Reviewers act; every action is logged.
ReportA quarterly Board and Audit Committee view of control health, open findings, ageing and remediation status.
